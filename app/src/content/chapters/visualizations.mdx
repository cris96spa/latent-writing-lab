## Explore the native visualization workflow

These examples use the research article template's own HTML/D3 embeds [@research-template].
They demonstrate the authoring mechanism: scoped HTML and styles, JavaScript interactions,
SVG graphics, and the template's shared theme. They are illustrative examples, not
evidence about a trained model.

Create an embed in `app/src/content/embeds/` and reference it with `HtmlEmbed` in a
chapter. The native template supplies the surrounding card, caption, theme and
download controls. Its article pipeline supplies math, citations, the table of contents,
and publication exports.

The [standalone function plotter](/figures/function-plotter/) demonstrates how a host
site can reuse a visualization without rewriting it in the host's component framework.
